# What the in-game help teaches and the wiki does not

Sources: the `Tutorial*` strings in `extracted/help/in-game-help.md`, and the
`Help*` strings in `extracted/assets/assets/lang_en.json`, which are the INFO
screen's own articles (`HelpDangP1-3`, `HelpDiscovP1-4`, `HelpPowerP1-2`,
`HelpBountyP1-2`, `HelpCargoP1-3`, `HelpMissionP1-3`) and are not in
`in-game-help.md`. Two further prefixes carry explanatory text and are already
used by the wiki: `ModuleSubType*Description` (module descriptions, quoted
throughout [[Modules]] and [[Combat]]) and the star and planet `*Desc` strings
(flavour, quoted in [[The galaxy]]).

## Contradicted

### Engine priority and handling

* Game: "Priority on engines also increases the acceleration and maneuverability of the ship." (TutorialPowerT)
* Wiki, [[Ship stats]]: "Acceleration: Top speed (before engine pips) ÷ 4.5" and "Rotation: top speed (before engine pips) ÷ 10".

The wiki is right. Acceleration and rotation are derived from `SpeedMax`,
which the engine pip factor never touches
(`decompiled-all/scripts/objects/Ships/ShipInfo.as:680` and
`ShipInfo.as:685`); the pips are applied only to `SpeedMaxWithPriority`
(`ShipInfo.as:340`). Engine pips raise top speed and nothing else.

### Systems priority and shields

* Game: "priority on systems increases shield strength" (TutorialPowerT)
* Wiki, [[Ship stats]]: "System pips cut damage to the shields to ×1, ×0.9, ×0.7, ×0.5 for 1 to 4 pips."

The wiki is right. `GetShieldsPipDmgKoefDecrease`
(`decompiled-all/scripts/objects/Ships/ShipInfo.as:755`) returns a damage
coefficient; shield maximum is computed without it.

### What raises the research rank

* Game: "Research rank is increased by completing tourist missions and selling research data." (HelpMissionP3)
* Wiki, [[Reputation and ranks]]: "Exploration rank: earned by '''selling discoveries''', and measured by the credits you have made from them in total."

The game is right and the wiki is incomplete. Completing a Tourist mission adds
its reward to `discoveryProgress`
(`decompiled-all/scripts/ui/screens/MissionsScreen.as:316-318`), the same
counter that selling discoveries feeds
(`ui/screens/DiscoveriesPortMenu.as:225`).

### Dropping a passenger on the way

* Game: "Tourist missions involve delivering a tourist to a certain system. The main thing is not to forget to bring him back to the station." (TutorialText46)
* Wiki, [[Missions]]: "A hundred hours on the clock, and the passenger will ask to be dropped somewhere along the way."

The game is right about the consequence, which the wiki leaves out: taking the
drop-off offer sets the mission's `Complete` flag to false and jettisons the
passenger's pod
(`decompiled-all/scripts/universe/Managers/EventManager.as:521-527`). The
mission pays only if the passenger returns to the station that issued it.
